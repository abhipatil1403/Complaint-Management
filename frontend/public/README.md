- **Recommended format**: PNG with transparent background or square aspect ratio (e.g. 256x256 or 512x512)

  1. Browser Tab Favicon (`<link rel="icon" type="image/png" href="/logo.png" />`)
  2. Apple Touch Icon (`<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/logo.png" />`)
  3. AICTE OneDesk Sidebar Brand Mark (`AppShell`)

  5. Authentication & Sign In Hero Card (`SignInPage`)
